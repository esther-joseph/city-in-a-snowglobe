




# Privacy Policy for Snowglobe Weather

**Last updated:** January 2026

“Snow Globe City” (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

---

## Information We Collect

“Snow Globe City” does **not** collect, store, or sell personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.

The app may access:
- **Location data (optional)** — used only to display local weather forecasts. Location access is optional and can be disabled in device settings.
- **App usage data (non-personal)** — basic analytics such as feature usage or performance metrics may be collected to improve app stability and user experience.

---

## How Information Is Used

Any data accessed by the app is used solely to:
- Display weather information
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Fix bugs and enhance features

Data is **not** used for profiling or identifying individual users.

---

## Third-Party Services

“Snow Globe City” may use third-party services that process limited data in accordance with their own privacy policies, including:
- Weather data providers (e.g., OpenWeather)
- Platform services (Google Play services)

These services are used strictly to support core app functionality.

---

## Advertising (Future Use)

The current version of “Snow Globe City” does **not** display advertisements.

If advertising is added in a future release:
- Ads will comply with Google Play policies
- An ad-free option will be available
- This Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly

---

## Children’s Privacy

“Snow Globe City” is **not designed specifically for children** but is safe for general audiences.  
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## Data Security

“Snow Globe City” does not store personal user data on external servers. Any data processed is handled using industry-standard security practices provided by platform services.

---

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
